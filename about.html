About Us

We roast coffee we'd want to drink ourselves.

Ember Roasting Co. started in a one-car garage with a 1kg sample roaster and a spreadsheet of farm contacts. Nine years later we roast out of a small warehouse in Portland, still on the same principle: buy well, roast honestly, ship fast.

Our Timeline

How we got here

01

2017 — First bag

Founder Alex Mercer roasted the first batch of Ember coffee on a converted popcorn machine and sold it at a Saturday farmers market.

02

2019 — First direct-trade contract

We signed our first multi-year contract with a farm in Huila, Colombia, guaranteeing a price floor regardless of the commodity market.

03

2022 — Moved to our current roastery

Outgrew the garage. Installed a 12kg drum roaster and started cupping every batch before it ships.

04

2026 — Eleven farms, four countries

Today we work with eleven partner farms across Colombia, Kenya, Ethiopia and Indonesia, and roast six days a week.

What we believe

Three things we won't compromise on

  • Every farm relationship is a multi-year contract, not a one-off spot buy.
  • No bag sits in a warehouse — we roast to order and ship within 48 hours.
  • Every roast is logged and cupped, so a bag you buy today matches the one you buy next month.
